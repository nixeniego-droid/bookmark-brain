# Grants & funding — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** grants for startups, non-dilutive funding, accelerators and incubators, pitch
competitions, government programs, and free credit programs (cloud, AI API credits).

**What I want out of it:** never miss a deadline on something I could actually apply for.

**For every save here, also fill these fields in the raw note's frontmatter** (leave blank if the
post doesn't say — never guess):
`deadline: <YYYY-MM-DD>` · `amount: "<as written>"` · `eligibility: "<who can apply, incl. country>"` · `apply_url: <link>`

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Upcoming deadlines:** a table sorted by deadline — program | amount | deadline | eligibility |
   link. Flag anything due within 14 days. Flag programs open to the Philippines / Southeast Asia
   or open worldwide.
2. **Rolling / no deadline:** programs that accept applications any time.
3. **Expired:** move past-deadline items here (keep them — many programs run every year).
4. **Be skeptical of:** "free money" posts with no official link, programs that charge an application fee.
