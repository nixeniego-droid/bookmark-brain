# AI & agents — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** AI in general — agents, new models, prompts, AI coding tools, AI image/video
tools, and assistants like Gemini or Perplexity. Tips for **Grok Bot**, **Claude** and **ChatGPT**
go to their own tips folders instead (link them here with `also:` if they matter for both).

**What I want out of it:** to know which AI tools and tricks are worth my time, and stop re-saving
the same things.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Tool tracker:** a small table — tool | what it does (≤5 words) | times saved | status
   (`new`, `try`, `tried`, `kept`, `dropped`) | newest save.
2. **Worth an hour this week:** up to 3 tools, prompts or workflows I can actually try, each linked.
3. **Hype check:** big claims with no demo, benchmarks with no source, launch-day posts. AI news
   goes stale fast — say how old each claim is.
