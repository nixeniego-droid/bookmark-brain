# AI & agents — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** everything AI — **Grok Bot**, **Claude** (Claude Code, skills, projects),
ChatGPT and other assistants, AI agents, new models, prompts, AI coding tools, AI image/video tools.

**What I want out of it:** to know which AI tools and tricks are worth my time, get the most out of
Grok Bot and Claude, and stop re-saving the same things.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Grok Bot & Claude corner:** the newest tips, features, skills and workflows saved for Grok Bot
   and Claude specifically — these come first.
2. **Tool tracker:** a small table — tool | what it does (≤5 words) | times saved | status
   (`new`, `try`, `tried`, `kept`, `dropped`) | newest save.
3. **Worth an hour this week:** up to 3 tools, prompts or workflows I can actually try, each linked.
4. **Hype check:** big claims with no demo, benchmarks with no source, launch-day posts. AI news
   goes stale fast — say how old each claim is.
