# My posts — what landed, in plain words

Optional, weekly. Read the vault's `AGENTS.md` first. Read only — never post.

1. Read the owner's own X posts (@nixeniego) from the last 7 days, with whatever numbers X shows
   (views, likes, replies, reposts, bookmarks). Use only numbers you can actually see.
2. Write `my-posts/<YYYY>-W<week>.md` with:
   - the 3 best-performing posts and one line on why each might have worked
   - what the owner posted about vs. what they **saved** most this week (see the latest review)
   - 3 post ideas from their saves they haven't posted about yet, each linked to the save
3. If a number is missing, leave it out. Never estimate.
