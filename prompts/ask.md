# Questions to try

Open your agent in the vault (or use the bookmark-brain skill in Grok Bot) and ask:

- What have I saved about AI agents? Newest first, one line each.
- Pick 3 things from my saves I can finish this week.
- Which AI tools have I saved more than once? Which one should I actually try?
- What business ideas keep showing up in my saves?
- What do I keep saving but never act on?
- Find two saves from different interests that solve the same problem.
- What do I save a lot about but never post about? Give me 3 post ideas in my voice.
- Which saves are 3+ months old and still `new`? Help me decide: try or drop.
- I tried <thing> — mark it done.
