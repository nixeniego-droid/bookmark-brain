# Marketing tools & automation — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** marketing software, marketing automations (n8n, Zapier, Make, AI workflows),
AI marketing tools, email/CRM tools, ad and creative tools, social and content tools — plus the
tactics people use them for.

**What I want out of it:** a clear picture of which tools and automations are worth setting up, and
ready-to-copy workflows.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Tool stack:** a table — tool | job it does | price if mentioned | times saved | status
   (`new`, `try`, `tried`, `kept`, `dropped`).
2. **Automations to build:** up to 3 specific workflows (trigger → steps → result), each linked to its save.
3. **Same job, different tool:** group tools that do the same thing and say which looks strongest.
4. **Be skeptical of:** affiliate-heavy "top 10 tools" lists, income screenshots, claims with no demo.
