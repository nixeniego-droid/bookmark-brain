# Tech startups — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** tech startups, startup ideas, founder lessons, building and launching products,
fundraising and investors (equity money — grants go in **Grants & funding**), startup news.

**What I want out of it:** ideas and lessons I can use while building, and a sense of where things are heading.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **In one line:** what I keep coming back to here.
2. **Ideas worth a closer look:** up to 3 startup ideas or opportunities, each linked, with why now.
3. **Lessons that repeat:** founder lessons that show up across several saves, 1–2 links each.
4. **Be skeptical of:** overnight-success stories, revenue claims with no proof, hype about a market
   with no numbers.
