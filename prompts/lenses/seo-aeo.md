# SEO & AEO — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** SEO (technical, on-page, content, links, local) and **AEO / GEO** — getting
found and cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Grok and Claude.

**What I want out of it:** tactics I can apply to a site this week, and to keep up as search shifts to AI answers.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **AEO first:** the newest saves on showing up in AI answers — what's claimed to work, with links.
2. **Checklist to apply:** up to 5 concrete changes (schema, page structure, content format, links…), each linked.
3. **Tools mentioned:** SEO/AEO tools saved, what each does, times saved.
4. **Be skeptical of:** "SEO is dead" takes, ranking claims with no data, tactics older than a year
   (search changes fast — say how old each claim is), and saves that contradict each other (link both).
