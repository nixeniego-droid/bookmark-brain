# Post ideas — how to read my saves (optional interest)

**What goes here:** posts I saved because I liked how they were written, or because I want to say
something about the topic myself.

**What I want out of it:** post ideas only I could write, rooted in what I actually care about.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Saved a lot, posted never:** topics I save most but haven't posted about (compare with `my-posts/`).
2. **5 ideas:** each a one-sentence stance in my voice, linked to the save that sparked it.
   My take, not a rewrite of someone else's post.
3. **Formats that work:** list, story, hot take, question — whichever got the most replies among my saves, if numbers are visible.
4. **One crossover:** a post that blends AI & tech with Business & marketing.
