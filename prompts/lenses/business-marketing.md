# Business & marketing — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** business ideas and models, offers, sales and clients, ads and ad creative,
copywriting, funnels, SEO, content and audience growth, founder lessons.

**What I want out of it:** plays I can run this week, and patterns in what I keep saving.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **In one line:** what I keep coming back to here.
2. **Plays to run:** up to 3 specific moves — a hook to test, an offer tweak, an outreach script,
   a page change — each linked to its save. Name the angle or format behind each.
3. **Patterns:** themes that show up again and again, with 1–2 links each. Point out ideas I keep
   saving but never try.
4. **Be skeptical of:** income screenshots, "I made $X in Y days", advice with no numbers or proof,
   and saves that contradict each other (link both).
