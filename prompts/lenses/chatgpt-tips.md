# ChatGPT tips — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** every tip, trick, feature, prompt and workflow for **ChatGPT** (ChatGPT, custom GPTs, OpenAI tools). A save
about ChatGPT goes here first, even if it also fits another interest (set `also:` for that one).

**What I want out of it:** a cheat sheet I can actually use to get more out of ChatGPT.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Cheat sheet:** the best tips grouped by topic (features, prompts, workflows, custom GPTs). One line
   each — what to do and what it gets me — linked to its save. Newest first within each group.
2. **Try this week:** up to 3 tips I haven't tried yet (`status: new`), each with a first step.
3. **New features:** anything saved in the last 30 days about a new feature or update.
4. **Might be outdated:** tips older than 6 months, or ones a newer save contradicts — these tools
   change fast, so say how old each tip is.
