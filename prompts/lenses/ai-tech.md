# AI & tech — how to read my saves

**What goes here:** AI tools and models, agents (Grok, Claude, ChatGPT and friends), prompts,
automations, coding tools and anything I could plug into my own workflow.

**What I want out of it:** to know which tools are worth my time — and to stop re-saving the same ones.

**How to write the brief at the top of the page:**
1. **Tool tracker:** a small table — tool | what it does (≤5 words) | times saved | status
   (`new`, `try`, `tried`, `kept`, `dropped`) | newest save.
2. **Worth an hour this week:** up to 3 tools, prompts or workflows I can actually try, and what
   each would replace in what I already use.
3. **Same job, new name:** saves that do what a `kept` tool already does.
4. **Hype check:** big claims with no demo, benchmarks with no source, launch-day posts.
   Model and pricing news goes stale fast — say how old it is.
